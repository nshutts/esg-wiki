# Novata ESG Standards and Frameworks Guide

ESG standards set specific technical requirements for measuring, reporting, and verifying sustainability data to ensure consistency and comparability. ESG frameworks, conversely, provide a broad, contextual approach defining themes and objectives for reporting, while standards prescribe exact metrics and methodologies.

## Key details

- ESG standards set specific and detailed technical requirements on how sustainability data should be measured, reported, and verified, creating consistency for comparison. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=1|Novata pp.1-2]])
- ESG frameworks provide a broad, contextual approach to ESG reporting by defining themes, objectives, and scope, guiding companies on topics, data collection, and presentation. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=3|Novata p.3]])
- ESG standards are prescriptive, defining exact metrics and methodologies, which is valuable for investors needing to compare performance across a portfolio or industry benchmark. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=5|Novata p.5]])
- ESG frameworks are broad and provide a contextual "frame" on how to disclose material topics, such as the TCFD, which leaves specific data points to the reporting company. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=5|Novata p.5]])
- The Global Reporting Initiative (GRI) Standards are known as the most widely used standards for sustainability reporting, with over 70% of leading companies globally adopting it as a primary framework. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=3|Novata p.3]])
- The Task Force on Climate-Related Disclosures (TCFD) was established by the Financial Stability Board in 2015 and is comprised of 11 recommendations structured around four pillars: governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics/targets. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=3|Novata pp.3-4]])
- The International Financial Reporting Standards (IFRS) Sustainability Disclosure Standards, including IFRS S1 and IFRS S2, were published in 2023 and are seeing rapid regulatory uptake, with over 36 jurisdictions committed to aligning with them. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata p.2]])
- The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a set of 17 interconnected global goals designed to address the world’s most pressing social, environmental, and economic challenges by 2030. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
