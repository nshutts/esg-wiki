# Australia AASB S2

AASB S2 mandates climate reporting in Australia based on IFRS S2, requiring specific components like governance, strategy, risk management, and emissions reporting. This is mandatory under Chapter 2M of the Corporations Act 2001 and applies to entities meeting certain size tests, with phased implementation for different groups.

## Key details

- Mandatory climate reporting is under Chapter 2M of the Corporations Act 2001, based on IFRS S2. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- The sustainability report must cover governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Scope 1 and 2 emissions are reported from year one, and Scope 3 emissions from year two. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Scenario analysis must be conducted at 1.5°C and at 2.5°C or higher. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Assurance phasing moves from limited to reasonable. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- In scope entities are those that lodge annual reports and meet two of three size tests, phased by group. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Group 1 began reporting years starting 1 January 2025 (revenue A$500m, assets A$1bn, 500 employees). ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Group 2 began reporting years starting 1 July 2026 (A$200m, A$500m, 250 employees). ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Group 3 follows from 1 July 2027 (A$50m, A$25m, 100 employees). ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Large emitters under the National Greenhouse and Energy Reporting Act and large asset owners are also included. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- Enforcement is handled by ASIC under the same civil penalty and director liability provisions as financial reports. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- A three-year "modified liability" period limits claims over Scope 3, scenario analysis, and transition plans to ASIC-only actions. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
