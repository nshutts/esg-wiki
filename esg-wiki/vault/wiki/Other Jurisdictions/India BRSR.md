# India BRSR

The Business Responsibility and Sustainability Report (BRSR) is India's mandatory ESG report for the top 1,000 listed companies, set by SEBI. It requires reporting on nine principles, with a subset called BRSR Core requiring independent assessment or assurance.

## Key details

- The Full BRSR covers the nine principles of India's National Guidelines on Responsible Business Conduct, spanning from environment to employee welfare to consumer fairness. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])
- BRSR Core is a subset of nine attributes that must be independently assessed or assured. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])
- BRSR Core assessment or assurance extends to all 1,000 listed companies from FY 2026-27. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])
- The Full BRSR is filed by the top 1,000 listed companies by market capitalization. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])
- Value chain disclosures for the top 250 companies are voluntary from FY 2025-26 after SEBI's March 2025 deferral. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])
- Listing obligation under SEBI's LODR Regulation 34(2)(f) requires filing the BRSR with the annual report. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])
- SEBI and stock exchanges can impose fines and other actions for non-compliance or misstatement. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])
