# Scope 1 2 and 3 Emissions

This note outlines the requirements for Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions reporting under various ESG frameworks and regulations. It highlights that different regimes mandate reporting based on their specific laws and standards, such as California's SB 253 and the ISSB standards.

## Key details

- California SB 253 requires annual Scope 1 and 2 emissions reporting starting from 2026, with Scope 3 reporting required from 2027. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- The GHG Protocol is the accounting rulebook for emissions that every regime in this briefing relies on, including the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance, and Scope 3 Standard. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing pp.1-2]])
- ISSB Standards (IFRS S1 and S2) require companies to report Scope 1, 2, and 3 emissions and climate scenario analysis. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing p.1]])
- Australian Sustainability Reporting Standards (AASB S2) mandate Scope 1 and 2 emissions from year one and Scope 3 emissions from year two. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing p.4]])
- SBTi focuses on setting science-based emissions reduction targets globally, which is a voluntary reporting requirement. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SBTi row]])
- CDP enables investors, companies, cities, states, and regions to assess greenhouse gas emissions and climate risks through a voluntary basis. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata p.4]])
- BRSR Core requires independent assessment or assurance for attributes including GHG emissions for the top 1,000 listed companies from FY 2026-27. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=4|Briefing pp.4-5]])

## Related notes

- [[ISSB Standards]]: IFRS S1 and S2 require Scope 1, 2 and 3 emissions.
- [[Australia AASB S2]]: AASB S2 requires Scope 1 and 2 from year one and Scope 3 from year two.
