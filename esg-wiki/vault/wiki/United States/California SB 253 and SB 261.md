# California SB 253 and SB 261

California has enacted two companion laws, SB 253 and SB 261, administered by CARB, which mandate corporate climate data and financial risk reporting. These laws require annual Scope 1 and 2 emissions reporting starting in 2026 and biennial public reports on climate-related financial risk.

## Key details

- SB 253 (Climate Corporate Data Accountability Act) requires annual Scope 1 and 2 emissions reporting starting from 2026 and Scope 3 reporting from 2027, measured under the GHG Protocol. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- Third-party assurance for SB 253 reporting is phasing from limited to reasonable. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- SB 261 (Climate-Related Financial Risk Act) requires a biennial public report on climate-related financial risk and mitigation, which may follow TCFD or ISSB. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- SB 253 applies to US-organized entities doing business in California with total annual revenue above $1bn. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- SB 261 applies to public or private entities with total annual revenue above $500m. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- CARB moved the first SB 253 deadline from 10 August to 10 November 2026 and will not require assurance on that first report. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- CARB administrative penalties under SB 253 are up to $500,000 per reporting year, and under SB 261 are up to $50,000. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
- Scope 3 misstatements are protected by a safe harbor if made in good faith, and penalties for Scope 3 are limited to non-filing until 2030. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing pp.3-4]])
