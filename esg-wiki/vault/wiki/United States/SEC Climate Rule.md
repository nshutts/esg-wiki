# SEC Climate Rule

The SEC's 2024 climate disclosure rule is currently stayed, meaning it has not taken effect. This rule was stayed in 2024, and the SEC stopped defending it in March 2025, with a proposal to rescind it on 29 May 2026. Until a final rescission, the 2010 interpretive guidance on material climate risk remains applicable. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing p.3]])
