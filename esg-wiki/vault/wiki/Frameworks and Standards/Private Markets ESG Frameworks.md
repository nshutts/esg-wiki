# Private Markets ESG Frameworks

Invest Europe and EDCI are frameworks designed for private equity and venture capital to address the fragmented approach to sustainability data collection and reporting. They aim to drive convergence around sustainability metrics to enable comparable reporting.

## Key details

- The ESG Data Convergence Initiative (EDCI) is a global private markets initiative focused on streamlining the industry’s approach to collecting and reporting sustainability data. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
- The core metric categories reviewed annually by the EDCI currently include greenhouse gas emissions, diversity, and employee engagement. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
- The Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a set of 17 interconnected global goals designed to address the world’s most pressing social, environmental, and economic challenges by 2030. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
- Invest Europe is a voluntary, principles-based ESG framework developed for European firms in the private equity, venture capital, and infrastructure sectors, and their investors. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata p.4]])
- Invest Europe provides guidance on integrating ESG considerations into the entire investment lifecycle, from evaluation to exit. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata p.4]])
- Invest Europe manages an ESG reporting template for reporting at the portfolio company, GP, and fund levels. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata p.4]])
