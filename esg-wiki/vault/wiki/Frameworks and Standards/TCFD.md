# TCFD

The Task Force for Climate-Related Disclosures (TCFD) was established by the Financial Stability Board in 2015 to provide recommendations for climate-related financial information disclosure. It guides companies in being more transparent about climate-related risk exposures to investors, lenders, and insurance underwriters.

## Key details

- The TCFD is comprised of 11 recommendations structured around four pillars: governance, strategy, risk management, and metrics/targets. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=3|Novata pp.3-4]])
- The TCFD was developed to guide disclosure recommendations for climate-related financial information. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=3|Novata pp.3-4]])
- TCFD is focused on climate-related financial risk. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, TCFD row]])
- TCFD emphasizes the financial impact of climate risks and includes scenario analysis. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, TCFD row]])
- TCFD is a voluntary reporting requirement but is increasingly adopted in mandatory disclosure regimes. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, TCFD row]])
- TCFD-aligned disclosures are now mandatory in many jurisdictions globally, including the UK, Japan, and New Zealand. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=3|Novata pp.3-4]])
- The TCFD disbanded in October 2023, but the IFRS foundation incorporated the full TCFD recommendations in its publication of IFRS S1 and S2 standards. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=3|Novata pp.3-4]])
