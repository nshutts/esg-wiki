# UN Sustainable Development Goals

The UN Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) are a set of 17 interconnected global goals established to address the world’s most pressing social, environmental, and economic challenges by 2030. They serve as a high-level framework for aligning business strategy and capital allocation with broader societal outcomes.

## Key details

- The SDGs are a set of 17 interconnected global goals. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
- The SDGs are designed to address the world’s most pressing social, environmental, and economic challenges by 2030. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
- The SDGs span areas such as climate action, clean energy, gender equality, and responsible consumption. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
- For companies and investors, the SDGs serve as a high-level framework for aligning business strategy and capital allocation with broader societal outcomes. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata pp.4-5]])
