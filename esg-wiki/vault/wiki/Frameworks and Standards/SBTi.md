# SBTi

The Science-Based Targets initiative (SBTi) is a framework focused on setting science-based corporate emissions reduction targets. It requires companies to commit to setting targets aligned with scientific goals, such as limiting global warming to 1.5°C.

## Key details

- Focus: Climate goals, GHG emissions reductions. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SBTi row]])
- Scope: Global. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SBTi row]])
- Reporting Requirement: Voluntary; companies commit to setting science-based emissions reduction targets. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SBTi row]])
- Key Features: Focuses on aligning corporate emissions reduction targets with scientific goals (e.g., limiting global warming to 1.5°C). ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SBTi row]])
- Key Takeaways: Focuses on climate science. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table]])
- Key Takeaways: Focuses on industry-specific guidance and emissions targets, with emphasis on climate science. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table]])
