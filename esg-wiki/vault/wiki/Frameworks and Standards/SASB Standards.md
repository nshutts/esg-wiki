# SASB Standards

SASB standards are industry-specific, voluntary disclosure standards focused on financially material sustainability issues relevant to investors. They are developed by the Sustainability Accounting Standards Board and aim to identify the most relevant ESG topics for each industry.

## Key details

- SASB focuses on industry-specific ESG performance. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SASB row]])
- SASB developed industry-specific disclosure guidance on sustainability-related risks and opportunities covering 77 industries. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata p.2]])
- The standards are designed to identify the most relevant environmental, social, and governance issues that may impact the financial performance and overall enterprise value of an organization. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata p.2]])
- SASB has an open standard-setting approach, allowing flexibility to determine what ESG topics are financially material to a business. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata p.2]])
- SASB focuses on financially material sustainability issues relevant to investors. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SASB row]])
- SASB standards set specific and detailed technical requirements on how sustainability data should be measured, reported, and verified. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=1|Novata pp.1-2]])
- SASB is aligned with TCFD and GRI. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, SASB row]])
