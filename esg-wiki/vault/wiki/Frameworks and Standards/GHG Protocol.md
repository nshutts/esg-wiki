# GHG Protocol

The GHG Protocol is the accounting rulebook for emissions that every regime in the briefing relies on. It consists of the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance (location- and market-based methods), and the Scope 3 Standard covering 15 value chain categories.

## Key details

- Components include the Corporate Standard, Scope 2 Guidance (location- and market-based methods), and the Scope 3 Standard (15 value chain categories). ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing pp.1-2]])
- A revision under consultation would require hourly and regional matching of renewable energy certificates for market-based Scope 2 claims. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing pp.1-2]])
- A final standard is expected in 2027, with implementation likely from 2028. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing pp.1-2]])
- It is an in-scope standard for Voluntary reporting. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing pp.1-2]])
- It is referenced directly by California SB 253, ISSB, AASB S2, and ESRS. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing pp.1-2]])
- Enforcement is not inherent; errors are penalized through the laws that reference it. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=1|Briefing pp.1-2]])
