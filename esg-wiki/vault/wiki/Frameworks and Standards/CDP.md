# CDP

CDP, formerly the Carbon Disclosure Project, is an international nonprofit organization that offers a leading environmental impact disclosure system. It allows various entities to assess greenhouse gas emissions and climate risks, helping to drive emissions reductions and strengthen climate resilience.

## Key details

- CDP is an international nonprofit organization that provides the world’s leading environmental impact disclosure system. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata p.4]])
- It enables investors, companies, cities, states, and regions to assess their greenhouse gas emissions and climate risks, driving emissions reductions and strengthening climate resilience. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata p.4]])
- CDP collects and standardizes environmental data on a voluntary basis to better inform corporate and city progress towards climate change, forests, and water security goals through an independent scoring methodology. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=4|Novata p.4]])
- Its focus is on environmental impact, particularly carbon emissions, with a global scope. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, CDP row]])
- Reporting requirement is voluntary, but it is used by investors and governments. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, CDP row]])
- It is aligned with SBTi, TCFD, and GRI. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, CDP row]])
- It focuses on environmental disclosures, especially climate and water security. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, CDP row]])
