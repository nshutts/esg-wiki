# Omnibus I Directive

The Omnibus I Directive (EU) 2026/470, in force since 18 March 2026, is a 2026 EU simplification package that reduced the number of companies in scope by roughly 80%. It also relates to changes concerning the Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) and introduces alternatives like the Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for non-listed SMEs (VSME). ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=2|Briefing p.2]], [[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])

## Key details

- The Omnibus I Directive (EU) 2026/470 is in force since 18 March 2026. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=2|Briefing p.2]])
- The Directive cut the number of companies in scope by roughly 80%. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=2|Briefing p.2]])
- Member States must transpose the Omnibus I Directive into national law. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=2|Briefing p.2]])
- The Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for non-listed SMEs (VSME) offers a streamlined, accessible alternative to the ESRS. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- The VSME helps SMEs report on sustainability performance in a way that is proportionate to their business needs. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- The VSME enables SMEs to demonstrate resilience, manage climate risks, and prepare for evolving regulations, aligned with the EU Omnibus I proposal and its “value chain cap” on sustainability data requests. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- The Corporate Sustainability Due Diligence Directive (CSDDD) requires compliance from 26 July 2029. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing p.3]])
- In scope for CSDDD, EU companies must have more than 5,000 employees and EUR 1.5bn net worldwide turnover, and non-EU companies must have EUR 1.5bn EU turnover. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing p.3]])
- The Omnibus narrowed the liability and penalty provisions for CSDDD. ([[raw/Global_Sustainability_Reporting_Standards_Executive_Briefing.pdf#page=3|Briefing p.3]])
