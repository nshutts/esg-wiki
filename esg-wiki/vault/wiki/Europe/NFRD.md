# NFRD

The Non-Financial Reporting Directive (NFRD) is a directive focused on sustainability-related disclosures for large companies within the EU. It is significant as it serves as the predecessor to the Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive (CSRD).

## Key details

- Full Name: Non-Financial Reporting Directive. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, NFRD row]])
- Focus: Sustainability-related disclosures for large companies. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, NFRD row]])
- Scope: EU. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, NFRD row]])
- Reporting Requirement: Mandatory for large EU public-interest entities. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, NFRD row]])
- Alignment with Other Standards: Aligned with GRI, TCFD. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, NFRD row]])
- Key Features: Predecessor to CSRD, focuses on non-financial reporting requirements in the EU. ([[raw/ESG_Frameworks.docx|Comparison table, NFRD row]])
