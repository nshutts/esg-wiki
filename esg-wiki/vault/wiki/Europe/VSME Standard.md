# VSME Standard

The Voluntary Sustainability Reporting Standard for non-listed SMEs (VSME) is a streamlined, accessible alternative to the ESRS designed to help small and medium-sized enterprises report on sustainability performance proportionate to their needs. It aids SMEs in responding to ESG demands from investors and corporate partners, thereby improving risk management and access to capital.

## Key details

- VSME offers a streamlined, accessible alternative to the ESRS. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- VSME helps SMEs report on sustainability performance in a way that is proportionate to their business needs. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- The VSME helps SMEs respond to ESG demands from investors and corporate partners in their value chain. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- The VSME helps SMEs improve risk management and access to capital. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- Companies have the flexibility to report based on what’s relevant and their reporting readiness through Basic and Comprehensive modules. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- VSME is aligned with the EU Omnibus I proposal and its “value chain cap” on sustainability data requests. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
- VSME enables SMEs to demonstrate resilience, manage climate risks, and prepare for evolving regulations. ([[raw/Novata_news.pdf#page=2|Novata pp.2-3]])
