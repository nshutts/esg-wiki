# ESG Reporting Wiki

A personal wiki on ESG frameworks, standards and regulations, built from three sources and written by a local Gemma model. Start with a topic below, follow its related notes, and use each note's source references to open the original page.

Notes are generated, then reviewed by hand; a note's `reviewed` property shows whether that has happened. Originals live unchanged in `raw/`.

## Frameworks and Standards

_Voluntary global baselines that most mandatory regimes build on._

- [[ISSB Standards]]: IFRS S1 and S2, the investor-focused global baseline.
- [[GRI Standards]]: The most widely used impact-focused reporting standards.
- [[SASB Standards]]: Industry-specific, financially material disclosure standards for 77 industries.
- [[TCFD]]: Climate-related financial disclosure recommendations, now carried by the ISSB.
- [[CDP]]: Global environmental disclosure system, formerly the Carbon Disclosure Project.
- [[SBTi]]: Science-based corporate emissions reduction targets.
- [[GHG Protocol]]: The emissions accounting rulebook every regime in the sources relies on.
- [[UN Sustainable Development Goals]]: 17 global goals used as a high-level framework for strategy.
- [[Private Markets ESG Frameworks]]: Invest Europe and EDCI, frameworks built for private equity and venture capital.

## Europe

_EU laws and the standards that implement them._

- [[CSRD and ESRS]]: The EU's mandatory sustainability reporting law and its technical standards.
- [[NFRD]]: The Non-Financial Reporting Directive, predecessor to CSRD.
- [[EU Taxonomy]]: Classification system for environmentally sustainable economic activities.
- [[SFDR]]: Sustainability disclosure rules for EU asset managers and financial products.
- [[CSDDD]]: EU due diligence duty on human rights and environmental impacts.
- [[Omnibus I Directive]]: 2026 EU simplification package that narrowed CSRD and CSDDD.
- [[VSME Standard]]: Voluntary sustainability reporting standard for non-listed SMEs.

## United States

_Federal and California climate disclosure rules._

- [[California SB 253 and SB 261]]: California's emissions (SB 253) and climate risk (SB 261) reporting laws.
- [[SEC Climate Rule]]: The stayed 2024 federal climate disclosure rule.

## Other Jurisdictions

_Mandatory regimes outside the US and EU that appear in the sources._

- [[Australia AASB S2]]: Mandatory Australian climate reporting based on IFRS S2.
- [[India BRSR]]: India's mandatory ESG report for the top 1,000 listed companies.
